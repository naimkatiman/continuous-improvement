What the audit checks.

35 checks in 8 groups. Each finding in your report cites its check ID.

  • PPermissions 6 checksBypass modes, shell-wide allow rules, missing deny rules for force push and rm -rf, unattended permission skips.
  • HHooks 7 checksGuard scripts that point nowhere, crash on empty input, print invalid JSON, or have no timeout, and hooks switched off.
  • MMCP servers 5 checksAuto-approved project servers, write tools with no deny rule, literal secrets, unpinned packages.
  • RUnattended runs 5 checksScheduled, headless and CI jobs with no turn limit, no kept output, no alert on failure, or a host that can kill them.
  • IInstructions 4 checksOversized or conflicting CLAUDE.md files, broken imports, and rules that claim enforcement that does not exist.
  • VVerification 3 checksNo test or typecheck command, no named proof of done, no CI on the agent's pull requests.
  • GGit 2 checksAn unprotected default branch, and several sessions sharing one checkout.
  • SSecrets 3 checksLiteral secrets in settings or MCP config, uncommitted-file hygiene, and secrets in agent logs.
